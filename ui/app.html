ShareMile

Version · MIT License

A tiny Windows app for sharing files over the internet at full line speed: a link or QR code for anyone with a browser, plus private share networks for your crew. Files travel straight from your PC to the receiver (or through an optional relay). Nothing is uploaded to a cloud drive or stored on anyone's server.
